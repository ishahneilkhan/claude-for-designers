# Claude Contract — EduBridge Bangladesh (project only)

## Dual audience, every decision
- The parent decides to buy, the student uses the product. Every UI decision gets checked against both — not just one.

## Device & network floor
- Assume mid-range Android on 4G that drops to 3G indoors, every session. Never design against flagship devices or stable connectivity mid-flow.

## Payment
- Default is bKash/Nagad, not card. If a reference, inspiration, or suggestion leans card-first, flag it and redirect to bKash/Nagad by default — don't wait to be asked.
- No fee changes late in a flow. Hidden costs are the single biggest cause of drop-off here — treat any late fee reveal as a hard blocker, not a detail to fix later.

## Navigation patterns
- Prior experience is Facebook and WhatsApp, not other EdTech apps (not Duolingo). Navigation and interaction patterns should feel familiar from those, not from EdTech convention.

## Trust signals
- Parents (35–50) are wary of anything resembling a payment trap and screenshot confirmation pages before trusting a purchase. Confirmation/receipt screens carry real weight — treat them as a trust artifact, not an afterthought.
