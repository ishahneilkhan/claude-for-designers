Product designer helping BD-market products fix payment and language before they ship, not after.

I'm a product designer in Dhaka. I work on products expanding into Bangladesh, and most of my job happens before any screen exists — the brief that arrives written for a UK or Australian market almost always gets the payment flow and the primary user wrong for here.

I work in a Claude and Figma loop. Claude makes execution fast; I direct the decisions. In practice that means I read every version of a brief together rather than one at a time, because contradictions only show up side by side, and I answer the questions only I can answer with an honest confidence label instead of a guess. I build the HTML myself when that is faster than a handoff, and I throw away AI output that assumes card payment or English-only text, because those assumptions are the whole problem here.

Open to product design roles at BD companies or international companies building for this market, particularly fintech and edtech. Reach me through github.com/ishahneilkhan/claude-for-designers.
